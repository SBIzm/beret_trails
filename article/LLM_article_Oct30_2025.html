The AI Assistant is Getting Smarter: What to Expect from LLMs in the Near Term


Remember when we first started playing around with things like ChatGPT? It felt a little like magic, but sometimes it also felt a little... clunky? Large Language Models (LLMs)—the technology powering tools like ChatGPT and Google’s Gemini—have already changed how many of us work and learn. For those who aren't tech experts, the next twelve months promise an even more interesting experience, moving these tools from novelties to essential, everyday assistants. We will think of them as less magic trick and more reliable entity to answer our questions and help us with daily tasks.

Near-Term Expectations for LLMs

Over the next year, you'll likely notice LLMs becoming better listeners and more reliable partners.
Goodbye, Silly Mistakes: One of the most frustrating issues—where LLMs confidently make up facts (often called "hallucinations")—will likely improve significantly. You'll notice this happens a lot less often. The models are getting much better at checking their work and will even start showing you exactly where they got their information, which will be a huge boost to your confidence in using them

The Rise of the AI Agent: This is the game-changer. Right now, you ask an AI to write something. Soon, you'll ask it to do something. Imagine saying, "Hey, book me the cheapest flight to see my sister next month, but make sure it’s a morning flight." The AI will connect to booking sites and handle the whole multi-step task itself. It will literally become truly becoming a personal assistant that manages your email, calendar, and travel plans.

More Specialized Tools: You'll also probably see AI tools designed for very specific jobs. Instead of one general-purpose chat, you'll have an AI tool designed just for legal inquiries, another for medical questions, and another for financial analysis. These focused tools will offer far better accuracy and expertise in their narrow field.


  • The AI Encroachment on Computer Search

The way you look things up on the internet is changing fast, and it’s all thanks to AI. Search engines are basically putting an LLM right at the top of the page to give you a direct answer instead of a list of blue links.
This is a double-edged sword: The Upside: If you just need a quick fact—"What's the capital of Chile?" or "How do I change a flat tire?"—you get the answer instantly, saving you time from clicking through a bunch of sites.,br> The Downside for Websites: When the AI gives you the answer directly, you don't actually visit the website that provided the original information. This means many online businesses will see fewer clicks and will have to rethink how they get noticed. Expect to see search results feel much more conversational, almost like asking a very knowledgeable friend.

  • Expanding AI Use in Schools and Businesses

Both education and the corporate world are embracing LLMs as tools for productivity and personalization. Businesses and schools are no longer asking if they should use AI, but how.

At Work: Forget simple chatbots. Companies are integrating it into their daily operations to perform tasks like summarizing long meetings, drafting internal reports, and analyzing vast amounts of data to spot trends. The goal is simple: let the AI handle the tedious stuff so employees can focus on the big, creative problems. Expect to see LLM tools become a standard part of software suites companies buy or lease.

In Schools: Teachers are using LLMs to create personalized learning. Think about an AI tutor that adapts to your child’s learning needs and, offering extra practice until they master a concept. While the conversation about cheating will continue, schools are quickly shifting to teach students how to use AI as a powerful learning aid, just like a calculator or research assistant.

The Takeaway:

  • The bottom line for the next year? AI is getting out of the sandbox and into your daily routine. It's going to be less about a single tool and more about a set of smart features woven into the apps and services you already use, making your life feel a little smoother and a lot more efficient,br>
